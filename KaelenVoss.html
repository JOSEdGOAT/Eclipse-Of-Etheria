Role: Central historical figure / Father of Lyra / Creator of the Aethel-Core
Race: Aurelian
Occupation: Grandmaster Scholar
Voss was the greatest scholar of the Aurelian civilization.
He discovered the spreading Abyssal and became obsessed with stopping it because his daughter, Lyra, was being consumed by it.

Major creation

Aethel-Core
Purpose:
  • Pour infinite Lumina into reality
  • Repair the universe
  • Stop the Abyssal
  • Save Lyra
His experiment caused:

THE RUPTURE
The explosion:
  • Destroyed the old world
  • Fractured space and time
  • Mixed Lumina with Abyssal
  • Created Eclipse Magic
  • Destroyed Aurelian civilization
  • Scattered Voss into countless Echoes
Important truth
Voss wasn't seeking power.
He was trying to save his daughter.
His attempt accidentally caused reality itself to carry fragments of Lyra.
Current form: Ghost/Echo
Final role: Final-story figure connected to the Cosmic Loom

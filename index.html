About

Dive into a world of magic and chaos in this thrilling game of survival. Help Aeturnus discover the truth of his birth and how he is tied to be the one who saves reality. Participate in dangerous missions and side quests. Learn how to use different types of magic and acquire weapons along your journey. You can also engage in multi-player fun with friends and people all around the world. It really is a fun game to play, so download and join the fun.

How To Play

For PC:

  • W = Move forward
  • A = Move left
  • S = Move backward
  • D = Move right
  • Esc = Back
  • E = Interact

For PS/XBOX:

  • Arrow Keys = Move
  • L = Movement
  • R = Camera direction
  • L1 = Lock on/swap
  • R1 = Heavy attack
  • L2 = Block/Guard
  • R2 = Special move

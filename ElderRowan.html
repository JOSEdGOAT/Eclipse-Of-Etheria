Role: Village Elder / Aeturnus's adoptive grandfather figure

Location: Elderglen Village

Rowan discovered Aeturnus 17 years ago inside a strange black crystal.

He raised Aeturnus like his grandson.

Known information

  • Knows more about Aeturnus than he initially admits
  • Protects Aeturnus
  • Knows about his mysterious origin
  • Possesses a fragment of the black crystal
  • Gives Aeturnus the ancient pendant
Story function:
He represents Aeturnus's connection to his ordinary childhood and acts as one of the first people to reveal the truth about his origin.
